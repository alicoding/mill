# Bookmark

Keeps a web address on the board and opens it in your browser. The
address is edited right on the card; Open asks Mill before it reaches
your browser.

## Settings

- **Title** -- what a bookmark shows as its title: the site name or the
  full address.
- **Title before an address** -- shown until you enter an address.

## Capabilities

- `open-url` -- opens the entered address in your default browser, on
  your approval.

## Try it

Copy the `mill-bookmark` folder into Mill's plugins folder (Settings >
Extensions > Open plugins folder) and reload plugins. Drop a link on
the board, or place a Bookmark from the tray and type an address.
