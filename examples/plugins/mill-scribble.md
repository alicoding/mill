# Scribble

A freehand drawing tool: drag on the board to draw a stroke that lands
as its own object, in the color and width you pick from its style
picker.

## Settings

None.

## Capabilities

None.

## Try it

Copy the `mill-scribble` folder into Mill's plugins folder (Settings >
Extensions > Open plugins folder) and reload plugins. Arm the Scribble
tool from the tray and drag on the board.
